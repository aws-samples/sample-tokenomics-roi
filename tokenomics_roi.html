AI Token ROI Workbench

Work backwards from business outcomes to token spend · coding agents in engineering and AI agents in customer support

1. Start from the outcomes the business cares about

Most customers care about one or more of the three outcomes mentioned below. The ROI for each outcome is evaluated on its own.

How the walkthrough goes

  1. Customer inputs (tab 2): customers, ARR, engineering and support headcount and cost, plus churn, features shipped and support contacts without vs with AI.
  2. Tokens & models (tab 3): how many tokens a feature and a support conversation burn, and which models do the work. This sets the token cost.
  3. AI adoption & spend (tab 4): how fast teams adopt AI, how extra features turn into customers, and the non-token spend.
  4. ROI results (tab 5): one card per outcome. Choose what the customer does with freed capacity: ship more, or spend less.
  5. How it was calculated (tab 6) and What-if (tab 7): show the math, then stress-test the drivers the customer is unsure about.
  6. Tokenomics (tab 8): practical ways to cut token cost and avoid throttling.
  7. Measure it (tab 9): the baseline metrics and tools that prove the gain after rollout.
